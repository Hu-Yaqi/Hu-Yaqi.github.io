research manuscript and projects

Rethinking Behavioral Drift: Non-Parametric Measures of Choice Inconsistency

2026

Hu, Y. Manuscript in preparation for journal submission.

  • Developed decision-boundary violations and matched flip scores to measure within-session changes in choice consistency under monotonicity constraints.
  • Used synthetic-agent simulations and within-participant permutation tests to evaluate apparent behavioral changes arising from model misspecification and stimulus composition.
  • Identified an early permissive-choice transient followed by stabilization in NARPS mixed-gamble behavior, with related patterns across five additional laboratory datasets and a smartphone dataset of 47,067 participants.
  • Found an association between inconsistent permissive choices and reduced dorsal putamen BOLD using trial-level behavioral and fMRI analyses.

How Do LLMs Read Our Minds? Magic, Human Accounts, and Ground Truth

2026–present

M.A. project, Media Studies and Production, Temple University; website and study design in progress.

  • Designing and building an interactive narrative website that performs a mind-reading trick, predicting each participant’s choice through a method set up in steps disguised as gameplay.
  • Designing a study in which large language models receive only participants’ written descriptions and explanations of the session; their answers are checked against session logs and the known method.
  • Developing tests of whether participants’ explanations help or hinder LLM inference and whether models distinguish observations from interpretations; also developing the project as a media artwork on living with opaque technologies.

What Do We Mean by Dating? Chinese Social Media and the Changing Language of Intimacy

2025

Course research project, Communication Research Methods, Temple University.

  • Adapted an open-source Python scraping framework to construct a structured corpus of 10,400 entries from Chinese social media platforms.
  • Built data-cleaning and text-preprocessing routines with pandas and NumPy; conducted exploratory topic clustering and keyword extraction.
  • Developed a preliminary thematic codebook to examine the language of intimacy; further analysis and interpretation are in progress.
